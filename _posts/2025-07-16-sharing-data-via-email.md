# Sharing data to route1.io via email

We’ve made it easy for you to send us your data just by emailing it.

## ✅ How It Works

1. **Tell us** you’d like to send data by email.
2. **We’ll create** a dedicated email address just for you, like:

   ```
   yourname@ingest.route1.io
   ```

3. **You send** your data (e.g., CSV, Excel files) as attachments to that email address.
4. **We take it from there.** Your attachments are automatically processed and securely stored in our system.

## 📎 Notes

- You can CC or BCC us—whatever fits your workflow.
- The subject and body don’t matter, we only process the attachments.
- You can send files as often as you like (daily, weekly, etc.).

That’s it, just email your files and we’ll handle the rest.
